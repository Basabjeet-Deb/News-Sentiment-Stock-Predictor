Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the predictions?

Our ensemble model achieves 49% accuracy with 56.8% F1 score on balanced test data. We believe in honest metrics, not marketing hype. This is NOT financial advice — use predictions as one signal in your overall strategy. Performance varies by market conditions.

What data sources do you use?

We aggregate news from 8 major sources: Reuters, CNBC, MarketWatch, Yahoo Finance, Google News, Barron's, Seeking Alpha, and The Motley Fool. Price data comes from yfinance API with real-time updates. All sources are publicly available and reputable.

Is my data secure?

Yes. The AI chatbot uses a local LLM (LM Studio) running on your machine — no data is sent to external servers. All processing happens locally. We don't collect, store, or share your queries or trading activity.

How often is data updated?

News is scraped continuously throughout trading hours. Predictions are regenerated daily after market close with the latest data. You wake up to fresh predictions every morning based on overnight news and updated technical indicators.

Do I need technical knowledge to use this?

No. You can chat with the AI naturally ("What are the top picks?") or browse the visual dashboard. No coding required. However, understanding basic stock concepts (sentiment, technical indicators) helps interpret results better.

Is this financial advice?

Absolutely not. MarketBrief is an educational research tool. All predictions are for informational purposes only and should not be considered financial advice. Always consult a licensed financial advisor before making investment decisions. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
